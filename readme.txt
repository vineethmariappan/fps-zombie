FPS Zombie Survival Wave Defense Game

Overview
This is a 3D first-person zombie survival game where players must survive increasingly difficult waves of enemies. Fight through waves of different zombie types, collect power-ups, and see how long you can survive in this action-packed survival shooter.

How to Play

Movement: Use WASD keys to move your character

Combat: Use left mouse button to shoot, 
Q for melee attacks, 
G to throw grenades

Camera Control: Move the mouse to look around

Sprint: Hold Left Shift while moving forward to run faster

Crouch: Hold C to crouch

Weapon Switching: Use scroll wheel or number keys (1, 2) to switch weapons

Pause: Press ESC to pause the game

Controls

W/A/S/D: Move character in respective directions
Mouse Movement: Rotate camera/aim
Left Mouse Button: Shoot current weapon
Right Mouse Button: Aim down sights
Q: Melee attack
G: Throw grenade
C: Crouch
Space: Jump
Left Shift: Sprint (while moving forward)
Scroll Wheel/1/2: Switch weapons
ESC: Pause menu
R: Reload weapon

Game Rules

Wave System

Survive waves of zombies with increasing difficulty
Each wave spawns more enemies than the last
Complete a wave by eliminating all enemies before time runs out
Time limit: 2 minutes per wave
Between waves: 25-second break period to collect power-ups

Enemy Types
The game features 4 distinct zombie types with progressive unlocking:

Wave 1:

Walking Melee Zombie: Slow-moving, close-range attacks

Wave 2+ introduces:

Running Melee Zombie: Fast-moving, close-range attacks
Walking Shooter Zombie: Slow-moving, ranged projectile attacks

Wave 3+ introduces:

Running Shooter Zombie: Fast-moving, ranged projectile attacks (most dangerous)

Power-Ups

Power-ups spawn between waves at random locations. Collect them for various benefits:

Health Sphere : Restores player health to maximum
Ammo Sphere : Refills ammunition (3 magazines worth)
Kill Sphere: Instantly eliminates all enemies on the map
Rocket Sphere : Doubles fire rate for 20 seconds
Random Sphere : Grants a random effect from the above

Health System

Player starts with 100 health and 3 lives
Taking damage depletes health
When health reaches 0, you lose a life and respawn after 5 seconds
Game over when all lives are lost
Your survival record (waves survived) is saved

Combat Mechanics

Shooting: Automatic and semi-automatic weapons available
Melee: Press Q for close-range knife attacks (150 damage)
Grenades: Limited supply, press G to throw explosive grenades
Recoil: Weapons have recoil patterns that affect accuracy
Aiming: Right-click to aim down sights for better accuracy

Ammunition

Limited ammo per magazine and reserve ammunition
Reload manually with R or automatically when magazine is empty
Collect ammo power-ups to replenish reserves
Cannot sprint while shooting or reloading
